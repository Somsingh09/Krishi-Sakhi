Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Krishi Sakhi ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, application, or use our digital agricultural services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the platform. This includes:

  • Personal Data: Name, mobile number, and location details (village, district, pincode).
  • Agricultural Data: Crop details, farm size, farming methods, and uploaded crop images for disease detection.
  • Marketplace Data: Transport requests, direct sales listings, and equipment rental inquiries.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Provide personalized AI-driven agricultural advice and crop disease detection.
  • Optimize logistics and routes for your Direct Sales (B2B/B2C) transport bookings.
  • Facilitate transactions and connections between farmers, buyers, and equipment owners.
  • Send targeted notifications regarding weather alerts and relevant government schemes.

3. Data Security & AI Processing

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information. Images uploaded for crop disease detection are processed by our AI systems to generate insights. We may use anonymized aggregated data to improve our machine learning models, but your individual farm data remains private.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share specific data (like crop availability and location) with verified bulk buyers (e.g., BigBasket) or logistics partners only when you explicitly request a connection or transport booking through our Direct Sales dashboard.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your information at any time through your dashboard settings. If you have any questions or require assistance regarding your data, please contact our support team.

By using Krishi Sakhi, you consent to our Privacy Policy. Together, we are building a safer, smarter digital ecosystem for farmers.
