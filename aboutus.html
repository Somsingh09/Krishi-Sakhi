About Us

Krishi Sakhi is India's most advanced digital companion for farmers, leveraging cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence to provide real-time agricultural advice, precise weather forecasting, and accurate mandi insights.


Our Mission

Our mission is to empower rural farming communities with accessible, reliable, and actionable information that directly improves crop yields and farmer livelihoods. We bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern technology.


Why Choose Us?

  • AI-Powered Insights: Instantly detect crop diseases with a single photo.
  • Real-Time Data: Get the latest weather forecasts and live Mandi rates.
  • Trusted by Thousands: Proudly built by Team Tech Spark for over 10,00+ farmers across India.
